FrankyRenderer 1.0.4 - Windows x64

INSTALL
1. Uninstall or unregister the original MPC Video Renderer first. FrankyRenderer
   currently uses the same registration for player compatibility. You do not
   need to uninstall MPC-HC or MPC-BE themselves.
2. Extract the complete ZIP to a permanent folder and close your video player.
3. Run Install.cmd and approve the Windows administrator prompt.
4. In PotPlayer or MPC-BE, select "MPC Video Renderer" in the video output
   settings. The loaded filter identifies itself as FrankyRenderer.

Keep the installed folder in its current location, including its DLLs and shaders.
The DLL files are the required third-party runtime libraries with descriptive
names. Their original names, source hashes and packaged hashes
are listed in licenses/runtime-names.json. Keep all of these files together.

OPTIONAL RTX SUPER RESOLUTION
Enable Super Resolution and choose its quality in the NVIDIA app or Control Panel.
Then enable RTX Video Super Resolution under Scaling in the renderer settings.
It requires a compatible NVIDIA GPU and driver and applies when upscaling video.
No additional RTX runtime files or helper process are installed.

UNINSTALL
Close your video player and run Uninstall.cmd from the installed folder.

SETTINGS BACKUP
Use Export in the settings menu to save an INI file. Use Import to restore it.
The backup includes all saved display profiles, disconnected devices and names.
Older single-profile INI files are imported into the selected display.
External ICC and LUT files must be kept separately; the INI stores their paths.

ABOUT THE SCRIPTS
Install.cmd and Uninstall.cmd start setup.ps1. It checks existing registrations,
backs up the affected entries and registers or unregisters the renderer. It
refuses to overwrite a registration pointing to another renderer installation.
